# Privacy Policy for Swaransh Academy App

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Swaransh Academy** mobile application (“App”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer (**“Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”**) for the purpose of academy administration, including admissions, student records, course management, and fee payments.

By using this App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

## 2.1 Personal Information Provided by You

When you submit an admission application or use the App, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Identity & Contact Details:** Full name, father’s name, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number, and residential address.
    
- **Academic & Course Information:** Education qualification, department, batch, subject, courses enrolled, learning mode (online/offline), admission type, start/end times, and fee details (amount and fee type).
    
- **Demographic Information (Optional):** Religion and caste (provided voluntarily for administrative/statistical purposes).
    
- **Profile Image:** A photograph uploaded for your student/admission profile.
    
- **Admission Status & Metadata:** Application status, timestamps of creation/updates, and related administrative notes.
    

## 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Google Sign-In Data:** If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address and basic profile information as permitted by your Google account settings and Google’s Privacy Policy.
    
- **App Usage Metadata:** Non-identifying technical metadata such as app version, session duration, and feature usage patterns to improve App functionality. We do **not** collect device identifiers, precise location, or advertising IDs.
    

## 2.3 Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests the following Android permissions:

- **Internet:** To communicate with our cloud database (Supabase) for admissions, profiles, and fee data.
    
- **Camera:** To capture and upload your profile photograph during admission or profile updates.
    
- **Notifications:** To send you important updates regarding your admission status, class schedules, fee reminders, and academy announcements.
    

You can manage or disable these permissions at any time through your device settings.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

- Processing and managing your admission application and enrollment.
    
- Maintaining student records, including course assignments, batch timings, and fee ledgers.
    
- Communicating with you regarding admission status, class schedules, fee payments, and academy updates.
    
- Improving App functionality and user experience.
    
- Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.
    

We do **not** use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose unrelated to academy administration.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

- **With Academy Staff:** Authorized faculty and administrative personnel of Swaransh Academy for admission processing, teaching, and student management.
    
- **With Service Providers:** Third-party vendors who assist us in operating the App and storing data securely (e.g., **Supabase** for cloud database hosting). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.
    
- **Future Payment Gateway:** When fee payment integration is enabled (e.g., Razorpay), payment-related data will be processed directly by the payment gateway in accordance with their privacy policy. We will not store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers.
    
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Swaransh Academy, our users, or others.
    

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information:

- All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using **HTTPS/TLS**.
    
- Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase) with access controls and authentication.
    
- Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
    

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you promptly in the event of a data breach affecting your information.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including:

- **Active Students:** For the duration of your enrollment at Swaransh Academy.
    
- **Former Students/Applicants:** Generally up to **1 year after you leave the academy or withdraw your application**, unless a longer retention period is required by law or for legitimate administrative purposes (e.g., fee reconciliation, audits).
    

After the retention period expires, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized.
